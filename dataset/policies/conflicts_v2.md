# Conflicts and Related-Party Transactions Policy

Policy ID: CONFLICTS | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Jurisdiction: Global

1. Directors, officers, employees, advisors, and their immediate family members must disclose interests in the target, sellers, intermediaries, or financing parties.
2. A disclosed related-party interest requires review by Legal and the Conflicts Committee before signing.
3. The conflicted person must not approve, negotiate, or sign on behalf of the company unless Legal documents an exception.
4. The transaction record must retain the disclosure, committee decision, and mitigation plan.
