# Sanctions and Restricted Parties Policy

Policy ID: SANCTIONS | Version: 2.1 | Effective: 2025-03-01 | Jurisdiction: Global

1. Corporate Development must screen the target, sellers, beneficial owners, lenders, and material intermediaries against applicable sanctions lists before signing.
2. A potential match requires Legal and Compliance clearance before signing or releasing funds.
3. No transaction may proceed where a party is confirmed to be a restricted party.
4. Screening evidence must record the screening date, data source, names searched, and reviewer.
