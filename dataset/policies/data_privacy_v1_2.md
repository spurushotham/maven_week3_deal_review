# M&A Data Privacy and Transfer Policy

Policy ID: DATA-PRIVACY | Version: 1.2 | Effective: 2025-02-01 | Jurisdiction: EU/UK

1. Personal data may be shared for due diligence only when the purpose, categories of data, recipients, retention period, and lawful basis are documented.
2. EU/UK personal data transferred outside the EEA/UK requires an approved transfer mechanism and documented security safeguards.
3. Special-category data must not be included in a diligence data room unless Legal approves the purpose and controls.
4. The review must identify deletion or return obligations after the transaction closes or terminates.
