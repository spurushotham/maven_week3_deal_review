# M&A Approval and Delegation Policy

Policy ID: M&A-APPROVAL | Version: 3.0 | Effective: 2025-01-01 | Jurisdiction: Global

1. Transactions with enterprise value above $50 million require approval from the Investment Committee before signing.
2. Transactions above $250 million require both Investment Committee approval and Board approval before signing.
3. The approval package must identify the purchase price, funding source, target ownership, material conditions, and expected closing date.
4. No employee may sign a definitive agreement before all required approvals are documented.
