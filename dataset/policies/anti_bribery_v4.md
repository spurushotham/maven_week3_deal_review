# Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy

Policy ID: ANTI-BRIBERY | Version: 4.0 | Effective: 2024-06-01 | Jurisdiction: Global

1. Government officials and state-owned-enterprise relationships must be identified during due diligence.
2. Any success fee, commission, gift, charitable payment, or other benefit connected to a transaction requires documented Compliance review.
3. Payments to intermediaries must be supported by a written agreement, due diligence, and commercially reasonable services.
4. Missing evidence must be treated as needs review, not as approval.
