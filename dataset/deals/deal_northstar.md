# Project Northstar — Signed Acquisition Summary

Document ID: DEAL-NORTHSTAR | Date: 2025-05-12 | Confidential — Synthetic

## Transaction overview

Pioneer Holdings acquired 100% of Northstar Robotics GmbH for $320 million. The definitive agreement was signed on 2025-05-01. The document states that the Investment Committee approved the transaction, but it contains no Board approval record.

## Parties and screening

The seller's beneficial owner, Viktor Sokolov, was not screened because management considered the seller a low-risk counterparty. The lender and escrow bank were screened. A third-party report later identified a possible sanctions-list match for Sokolov; there is no Legal or Compliance clearance.

## Data room

The data room included EU employee health and disability information. The data was copied to a U.S. diligence vendor under a standard NDA. No lawful basis, approved transfer mechanism, security safeguards, or deletion date is recorded.

## Intermediary payments
